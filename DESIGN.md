# Design System: Sprout Lab — Illustrated Adventure and Greenhaven

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Printed Garden Comic"**

An exuberant children's comic pairs oversized, friendly lettering with inked gouache garden scenes. Sprout and Beep invite curiosity; readable live text and clear controls keep the adventure usable by Year 4 pupils.

The user-approved illustrated world governs the established learning trail: map, mystery, story, leaf factory, experiment lab, creek, detective, challenge, finale, journal, settings, teacher resources, sources and safety, and the offline trail. Activity scenes are expressive; supporting pages are quieter reading pages from the same book. The rollout authority is `docs/full-site-direction.md`; `docs/first-screens-direction.md` records its approved origin. Sources are `dist/first-screens.css`, `dist/adventure.css`, their live markup, and the inherited styles they extend.

The approved Greenhaven extension is a separate, desktop-first live 3D forest at `#grove`; `#check` opens this assessment. It pairs supplied animated Nico and nature models with a small rooted procedural Sprout and creamy live HUD controls. The world follows the owner’s pinned Zelda-style environmental exploration direction: one continuous forest, an elevated camera and three connected clearings. Sources are `dist/grove.css`, `dist/grove.js`, `dist/grove-adventure.css` and the quest, progress and certificate modules. `docs/greenhaven-full-adventure.md` records the implemented expansion; `docs/greenhaven-scene.md` preserves the approved first-scene history. Prefix `grove-` scopes additional primitives and components to this surface; it does not replace the illustrated system. Ungraded creek practice leads into ten assessed decisions, local explorer profiles, separate attempts, results and named PDF certificates.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Painted garden scenes and one recurring illustrated cast.
- Dark ink, warm paper, leafy actions, sunshine and coral emphasis.
- Asymmetric scene corners, expressive headings and brief handwritten notes.
- Live learning text, controls and scientific diagrams beside the artwork.
- In Greenhaven, a live forest with quiet parchment HUD and one persistent continuation action.

## Colors

The palette combines warm printed paper and sunshine with botanical greens, powder-blue sky and dark coral.

- **Primary:** Leaf anchors continuation actions, checked controls, completed discoveries and links; leaf-hover deepens the main action.
- **Secondary:** Printed coral marks headline words and navigation underlines; soft coral is a distinct filled-action variant. Sunshine identifies experiments, current stages and earned badges. Focus coral marks keyboard focus.
- **Tertiary:** Warm and cool scene surfaces support the illustrated theatre, narrator, puzzle and quiz companion. Water and gas tints distinguish science controls. Choice-selected and switch-selected are separate existing selection surfaces; success and incorrect are assessment feedback.
- **Neutral:** Ink carries text and structural outlines; muted-ink carries captions. Paper is the shared page, field and speech surface. Reading is the gently tinted evidence/letter surface. Line and reading-line divide quieter sections.

The frontmatter records reused interface colors, not the complete painted image palette or every SVG pigment. Blue water routes, purple gas markers, green leaf cells and soil tones inside scientific diagrams are intentional subject encoding and retain their source geometry and colors.

### Greenhaven scope

Forest ink and parchment carry HUD reading and neutral controls. Translucent HUD and panel surfaces retain the warm parchment hue over the live world. Grove action green identifies forward/release actions, cream-hover lifts neutral controls, and a warm brown focus outline separates keyboard focus from the illustrated route’s coral. Disabled controls retain an opaque gray-green fill and readable muted ink. The nickname field uses the shared pale-cream field primitive. Three-dimensional foliage, water, timber, lighting and fog remain scene materials rather than interface tokens.

## Elevation & Depth

Ink outlines establish structure. Soft shadows lift actions, speech, earned badges, word tiles and the pipe board; the larger scene frames stay flat. The settings dialog has a diffuse shadow and a dim ink backdrop without blur. Exact shadows and motion are in the sidecar.

Shared control transitions last 180ms. Activity actions rise 2px on hover; the cover action rises 3px and turns slightly; prediction options shift sideways. Pressed buttons move down 2px. Reduced-motion preferences disable transitions and animation.

**The Scene Frame Rule.** Give an illustrated activity one coherent ink frame; use spacing and thin dividers for instructions, evidence and supporting reading sections.

### Greenhaven scope

The live 3D camera, terrain, soft scene shadows and fog provide environmental depth; translucent parchment and diffuse HUD shadows keep controls separate from it. Standard grove controls use the `grove-control` sidecar shadow; mission, discovery and modal layers have their own diffuse values. Buttons rise 2px on hover and move down 1px on press, with 180ms transitions; feedback fades and shifts over 200ms. Both the system reduced-motion preference and the saved reduction setting suppress CSS transitions/animations. Exact motion and depth values belong in the sidecar.

**The Forest First Rule.** In Greenhaven, keep controls as a quiet readable layer over the live environment; do not replace the playable scene with a dashboard or quiz-card grid.
