Reviewed 16 October 2026 against the supplied documents:

## Scope and conclusion

The website is a photosynthesis learning module. It supports the requested DSKP 4.2.1–4.2.4, not the complete Year 4 Science DSKP. Standard 4.2.5 is excluded from the assessment and alignment targets at the owner’s request. Optional reflection can remain without treating it as evidence for that standard.

The previous website already taught the meaning, four needs, glucose/oxygen, stored starch and benefits. Its glucose explanation was scientifically consistent with textbook page 78. The issue was emphasis: the DSKP names starch and oxygen, so the connection to stored starch needed to be more prominent. A teacher-page statement incorrectly identifying the supplied DSKP as Year 2 was also removed.

- Ten offline questions mirror the same curriculum targets. Timing is flexible; the game-based quest now uses flexible timing; no measured duration is promised.

## Assessment limits

Each challenge is one point if completed before a science hint. Wrong knowledge-check answers give explanatory hints. A conveyor science misclassification or catching the wrong gas also gives a science hint; the optional hint button is available in each game. Harmless wall collisions, mirror adjustments, railway tests and restarts do not reduce the score. The result is formative feedback, not a measure of reflex speed or an official performance-level award.

All games require their goal to be completed before Next unlocks. Mirrors, tracks, crate symbols and batch quantities are teaching devices, not extra curriculum requirements or literal plant mechanisms. The site does not fully teach or assess 4.1 plant responses, nor the real investigations and creative communication described in the broader performance-level descriptors.
