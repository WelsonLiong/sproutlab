# The Last Leaf integration plan

The third-person game is not implemented. Preserve the original prompt and supplied assets outside this Site. The present creek puzzle is an independent 2D mini-game.

## Proposed story improvements

Use a storm-damaged community nursery as the concrete cause, rather than vague environmental damage. Nico and Lumi repair one small ecosystem around rooted Sprout. Make Sprout an ordinary plant that matters to the community; avoid implying one magic tree can instantly repair an entire forest.

Condense the core episode to 5–7 minutes so it can eventually replace the three-minute creek segment plus some lab practice without turning a 15–20 minute module into a 30–40 minute game. Keep longer woodland exploration as optional enrichment.

## Proposed gameplay

1. Observe two visible clues: a blocked irrigation route and a fallen shade cloth. Use a field lens to inspect roots and leaves.
2. Predict which problem to fix first. Both orders work; feedback describes the changed condition.
3. Route water with gates, not a fetch-the-lever chore. Water travels downhill to roots.
4. Move the shade cloth with a short spatial puzzle. Do not introduce mirrors as a required photosynthesis mechanism.
5. Use the field lens to see an annotated leaf with chlorophyll, incoming carbon dioxide and water, and outgoing products. Airflow is not a separate biological requirement; do not imply wind is needed or carbon dioxide has disappeared from an open forest.
6. Complete a transfer problem at a second seedling with one different missing condition. Ask for an evidence-based intervention, not an out-of-world multiple-choice popup.

The plant makes glucose only after appropriate conditions are available. It does not eat collected sunlight, water or CO2. Growth animations use a labelled time-lapse transition, never instant biological growth. Oxygen also is not a health collectible. Plants respire in daylight and at night.

## Interaction and integration contract

Keep game state and controls within a separate route or same-origin iframe. Start only after the pupil chooses Play; do not load large 3D assets on the learning map. Provide keyboard remapping, pause, mute and low-quality settings. When implemented, require explicit completion data with a versioned message type, verify exact event.origin and iframe source, and accept only known quest IDs. Returning to the website preserves the learning trail and focus.

Review the supplied asset licenses before deployment. The nature pack Standard license and character/animation ownership require separate verification. Neither license acceptance nor future deployment is assumed by this document.
