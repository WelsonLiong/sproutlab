# Seven playable games and three checks — 16 October 2026

## What changed

The owner rejected the interaction-only quiz as a prettier form. Seven of the ten challenges were replaced with game mechanics that update a world state and detect a goal: a leaf resource factory, sunlight-mirror routing, a water maze, a Sokoban-style starch store, a moving conveyor gate, food-energy railway construction and CO₂ bubble patrol. Meaning, chlorophyll identification and importance/reasoning remain short checks. Target standards remain 4.2.1–4.2.4; 4.2.5 remains excluded.

Game 2 consumes water and CO₂ for each of three food batches and requires chlorophyll and a fully uncovered Sun. Game 3 traces the beam after mirror turns. Game 4 moves a water drop with arrows and collision rules. Game 6 pushes glucose crates into storage spots; stored crates become starch. Game 7 transports supplies down a selected conveyor branch. Game 8 traverses a connected, rotated track through plant, caterpillar and bird. Game 9 has moving bubbles, pause/resume, CO₂ capture and oxygen release.

## Scoring and access

The display records challenges completed before science hints. Incorrect direct answers, conveyor need/product confusions and accepting O₂ instead of CO₂ trigger science explanations. Wall bumps, mirror adjustment, track tests and puzzle resets do not cost points. Goals must be met before Next unlocks. All levels can be retried, with no timed failure. This is formative practice, not an official DSKP performance-level grade.

Touch movement buttons and keyboard arrows work in grid games. All rotations, gates and bubbles are native buttons. Focused bubbles pause their motion; a Pause control and reduced-motion settings also stop it. Game transitions are cancelled on challenge change and route cleanup. The single bottom forward button, locks, saved results and journal total remain.

Mirrors, food rails, crate conversion and batch quantities are game representations, not literal plant anatomy, chemical ratios or a separate reflection-of-light assessment. The supplied science sources and curriculum mapping remain in `curriculum-alignment-review.md` and the teacher page.

## Verification

An isolated Edge browser completed all ten challenges and verified all seven game goals. Negative checks covered an empty leaf, insufficient sunlight, an incomplete beam, a wall collision, a wrong conveyor destination, a disconnected food railway and an oxygen bubble. Goals remained locked until completed. The test deliberately used three science hints and confirmed a 7/10 hint-score result, then replayed. It checked the single Next control, desktop/390px/320px overflow, keyboard maze movement, reduced-motion operation and route cleanup. New game layouts were visually inspected in two bounded passes. The final pass corrected uniform mirror-grid rows and synchronised the Sun/cloud feedback. JavaScript syntax and whitespace checks passed.

Automated playthroughs verify behavior. Pupil/adult classroom usability and formal assessment remain separate evidence.
