# Sprout Lab — revised direction, 8 October 2026

A child-first cartoon garden adventure for English Year 4 Science, Unit 4 photosynthesis. The user rejected the first text-heavy interface as too mature; the redesign was built directly in code without continuing the Impeccable workflow.

The illustrated map is the working navigation. Sprout remains rooted; Beep is a fictional AI helper whose mistaken claim becomes a short evidence mission. A cheerful Sun and Water character support the diagrams. Chunky rounded controls, restrained hover movement, warm cream backgrounds and green, yellow, blue and lavender scenes establish the visual language.

Seven stations retain a 19-minute RPH sequence: induction, explanation, exploration, application game, AI investigation, assessment and closure. Detailed pedagogy and submission notes remain in Teacher’s corner. Each detective stage is revealed separately rather than stacked into a long worksheet.

The current games are two water-routing puzzles and an evidence/sentence/privacy mission. The leaf model supports prediction and comparison. A single five-question assessment provides explanatory feedback and retry; the opening prediction is ungraded.

The supplied third-person game is deferred. See future-game.md for a proposed learning-led redesign and integration contract. No supplied 3D asset is included.

## Teaching through action — 11 October 2026
After the user found the notes too textbook-like, added an alternative: a 36-second cartoon lunch mix-up, a 49-second worked demonstration, then a playable leaf factory. This teaches before expecting pupil action. Water travels up the roots and stem, carbon dioxide enters the leaf, and chlorophyll absorbs sunlight as energy. Production begins automatically when the model has its needs. Pupils follow glucose and oxygen to discover plant nutrition, starch storage and benefits to living things.

The original notes remain available for comparison. Both modes award the same Leaf explorer discovery and occupy the same 3-minute explanation slot. Counts and timing are illustrative; the factory is explicitly a metaphor, and it does not imply leaves contain mechanical machines. No new quiz, backend, third-party asset or third-person game was added.
